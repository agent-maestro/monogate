# MachLib Polynomial Workbench Explorer Surface

Status: internal prototype surface.

The Explorer IR tab now includes a MachLib polynomial evidence workbench panel
backed by the v21-v25 MachLib packets:

- v21 polynomial evidence workbench packet
- v22 Explorer root packet export
- v23 root-count target inventory
- v24 certificate validation bridge
- v25 eFrog polynomial adapter dry-run packet

The panel shows the default expression `x^2 - 15*x + 56`, the bounded v16
search result, the v18 quadratic residual classification, and the v19
certificate validation result. It also previews coefficient and factored
expression examples.

## Boundary

This is not a general root-count theorem, arbitrary root discovery engine,
Forge compiler change, eFrog behavior change, package publish, public theorem
claim, PETAL/API upload, Hugging Face upload, certified safety claim, or
production controller claim.

## Next

The next research bridge remains normalized product degree growth, root-list
union/cardinality, and replacing packet certificates with derived facts where
the tiny MachLib substrate can support them.
