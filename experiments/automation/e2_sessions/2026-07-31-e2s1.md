## Two disclosures that belong in the pre-registration, not in the write-up

**1. The selecting context is warm.** This target was chosen by an AI that had just spent six sessions
on EML asymptotics and had `EMLTreeValid.divisor_pos`, `inner_arms_coupled`, the totalization finding
and the `K/x` floor in working memory. That is not a violation — it is what an AI-selected target
*looks like*, and E2 exists to measure it. But if the `E2-ai` arm outperforms, "the AI picks targets
adjacent to what it just learned" is a live alternative explanation to "the AI selects well", and the
scoring must not get to choose between them silently.

**2. This is deliberately NOT the E5 target.** The E5 arm closed today at 6 of 6 with an explicit
"no further sessions without a fresh pre-registration and a fresh budget". Running `1/x ∉ EML` under an
E2 banner would be that arm spending a budget it was just denied, with the label changed. `x + 1 ∉ EML`
is a different open problem in the same object, with its own prior work and its own frontier note.

## Why this target and not another

Three candidates were considered from `grep`-level survey of the machlib frontier notes: the constructive
chain-length-2 bound over `IterExp 2` (`ChainExp2Instance.lean`, blocked on framework redesign — too
large for one session), the propagating-regularity circularity in `EMLPfaffian.lean` (same problem,
flagged as open in two separate files), and this one. This one was chosen because **it has a cheap
adversarial pass**: the E5 arm's repeated result is that trying to *build* the object first is the
highest-yield opening move, and `x+1` at depth 2 is small enough to attempt directly.
